   152→6. Inventory: the central business invariant
   153→
   154→Model availability per SKU, variant, stock location, and eligible lot.
   155→
   156→Define:
   157→
   158→Available to sell = sellable physical quantity - active reservations - configured safety stock.
   159→
   160→Sellable physical quantity excludes expired, damaged, quarantined, and otherwise unsaleable stock. Do not subtract the same held quantity twice.
   161→
   162→Mandatory behaviour:
   163→
   164→    When availability is zero, remove that SKU from all purchasable storefront lists and recommendations.
   165→    When every variant is unavailable, hide the product from normal storefront discovery.
   166→    A previously saved link may show a clear unavailable state, with purchasing disabled.
   167→    Revalidate availability and price at checkout and before completing a counter sale.
   168→    Browsing and adding to a cart do not permanently reserve stock.
   169→    Creating a checkout order reserves stock for a configurable period.
   170→    Reserve a whole multi-item order atomically, or return clear line-level conflicts without leaving stray holds.
   171→    Both online orders and POS use the same reservation and inventory service.
   172→    Use a single documented stock-depletion event. For delivery, dispatch can consume both physical stock and its reservation; for counter sales, completed handover consumes them together.
   173→    Picking and packing must not deduct physical stock again.
   174→    Cancellation releases unconsumed reservations. Cancellation after stock has left the store requires an actual return process before restocking.
   175→    Returned goods enter inspection or quarantine; only an approved saleable disposition makes them available again.
   176→    Selecting collection versus delivery must use an appropriate stock source. Do not invent an inter-location transfer.
   177→    Demonstrate a last-unit conflict in the mock service. Document that real multi-user concurrency requires a backend transaction or equivalent atomic constraint.
   178→    When authoritative stock cannot be verified, allow draft work but do not present a completed sale.
   179→
   180→Support configurable purchasing and selling units, such as carton, pack, piece, kilogram, and litre. Record explicit conversion factors. Track fractional quantities with decimal strings or scaled integers; do not use imprecise floating-point values as stock authority.
   181→
   182→Support lot numbers, expiry dates, goods receiving, supplier references, damage, wastage, and reasoned adjustments. Suggest first-expiring eligible lots for picking.

   190→Dimension       Example states

   192→Fulfilment      awaiting_confirmation, confirmed, picking, packed, dispatched, delivered, ready_for_collection, collected, cancelled
   193→Delivery        unassigned, assigned, accepted, picked_up, out_for_delivery, delivered, failed, rescheduled, return_to_store
   194→Return  requested, approved, rejected, received, inspected, resolved
   195→Refund  requested, awaiting_approval, processing, succeeded, failed, requires_review
   196→
   197→Display human-readable labels and a timestamped history. Enforce permitted transitions in the mock service; document backend enforcement requirements.
   198→
   199→Offer configurable Mobile Money, hosted card checkout, verified bank transfer, cash at counter, and cash on delivery. Hubtel is a candidate adapter for later integration, not an already connected merchant account.
   200→
   201→Payment safeguards:
   202→
   203→    A browser redirect or customer screenshot cannot mark an order paid.
   204→    The future backend must confirm provider outcomes using its supported verification mechanisms.
   205→    Include payment attempt identifiers and idempotency keys.
   206→    An unknown outcome stays pending or requires review; do not encourage a duplicate payment blindly.
   207→    Demonstrate a successful payment, failure, pending outcome, abandoned checkout, and duplicate callback.
   208→    A reservation expiry with an unresolved payment requires reconciliation.
   209→    If payment succeeds after the reservation has expired, recheck stock. Route an unsatisfied paid order to staff review and the supported cancellation/refund process.
   210→    Cash-on-delivery orders may be confirmed while payment remains unpaid.
   211→    Delivery completion, cash collection, and rider remittance are different events.
   212→    Keep provider secrets and card details out of the frontend.
   213→
   214→Money must use integer minor units or a documented decimal representation, with GHS as the currency code and a consistent visible ₵ format. The backend will calculate authoritative prices, discounts, fees, tax, and totals. Do not hardcode assumed Ghana tax rates.

   259→10. Returns, refunds, and exchanges
   260→
   261→Make returns and refunds first-class workflows for online and counter orders.
   262→
   263→Customers or staff select original order lines, eligible quantities, reasons, and evidence. Support full and partial returns, approval or rejection, receipt at the store, condition inspection, and a disposition.
   264→Disposition     Stock effect
   265→Saleable and approved for restock       Return to an eligible saleable lot or a properly recorded new lot
   266→Damaged, expired, or unsafe for resale  Remain unsaleable; record loss or approved disposal
   267→Needs inspection or supplier review     Keep quarantined
   268→Never physically returned       Do not increase physical stock
   269→
   270→Provide refund previews based on original paid amounts, allocated discounts, applicable tax, and the configured delivery-fee policy. Track cumulative returned quantities and cumulative refunds so neither can exceed the eligible original balance.
   271→
   272→Support refund approvals, reasons, provider references, retryable failures, and reconciliation. A refund request or approval is not a completed money transfer.
   273→
   274→Distinguish a payment-provider refund from an unrelated payout. Keep manual refund recording clearly identified and permission-controlled.
   275→
   276→Reflect authorised reversals in sales, stock, cost of goods where applicable, tax, cash/payment balances, and reporting. Document the authoritative backend ledger requirements.
   277→
   278→Model an exchange as a traceable return plus a new sale, with a linked price difference. Do not overwrite the original sale history.

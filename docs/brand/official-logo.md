# Variety Groceries — official visual identity

Approved owner-supplied logo (16 November 2026): a metallic gold **VG** shield-style monogram on the left with the bold black stacked words **Variety** and **Groceries.** on the right (including the final period).

## Repository assets

- `public/brand/variety-mark.svg`: scalable official VG monogram for favicons, compact views and staff/rider UI.
- `public/brand/variety-lockup.svg`: scalable horizontal full wordmark for storefront header/footer.
- `public/logo.svg`: compatibility alias for the official monogram.
- `deploy/coming-soon/logo.svg` and `deploy/coming-soon/variety-lockup.svg`: corresponding static landing page assets.

The source of truth is the owner-supplied logo, **not** the retired green V icon or earlier animated Z mark. New pages and app surfaces should reuse the existing official assets rather than redraw, recolor, or substitute another symbol. Use the black wordmark on white/light surfaces; on dark backgrounds, present the logo on a white backing panel to retain its contrast. Responsive presentations should preserve aspect ratio and not crop the logo.

A high-resolution enhanced PNG and transparent variants have also been prepared for content, social, documents and cross-platform use. Store their original production sources in the brand asset library; the repository SVGs are resolution-independent tracings for application chrome.

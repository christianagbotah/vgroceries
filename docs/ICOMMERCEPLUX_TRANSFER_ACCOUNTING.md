# iCommercePlux — Head-office-authorized transfers

The destination requests goods; head office chooses an active source (any
internal branch or warehouse) and authorizes quantities. A location grant,
even for both branches, is not head-office authorization. The current
foundation uses the existing admin role for head-office decisions, and
inventory_manager with location membership for stock-handling actions.
The next integration must load these facts from authenticated, persisted
records and check them again under transaction locks. The policy is not
an endpoint or database authority by itself.

## Accounting example (GHS)

100 cartons at original unit cost 25.00, total 2,500.00. No sales revenue,
supplier payable, VAT or new purchase is generated by the internal transfer.
Illustrative perpetual inventory control-account postings:

| Event | Debit | Credit | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Dispatch | Inventory in transit | Inventory, source branch | 2,500.00 |
| Accept 98 cartons | Inventory, receiving branch | Inventory in transit | 2,450.00 |
| Accept outstanding 2 cartons later | Inventory, receiving branch | Inventory in transit | 50.00 |

After the initial receipt the remaining two cartons and their 50.00 value
stay outstanding; missing, damaged or rejected stock needs independently
authorized resolution (return, recovery or justified loss/write-off).
A transfer must not silently close with unresolved quantity or value.
Separate actual freight/handling costs and any justified capitalization
under the company's accounting policy; never invent costs or markups.
Use the existing source lot's cost and expiry/batch provenance, not the
retail price. Unknown costs are blocked for valuation, not treated as zero.
The pure calculator uses integer thousandths and integer minor currency
units; cumulative rounding preserves the total across partial receipts.
Actual dispatch posting must reconcile source carrying value, including
fractional-lot rounding across multiple transfers and existing movements;
the calculator alone is not a full inventory costing engine.

## Integration gates

Persist a request, authorization, version, lines, lot allocations, immutable
dispatch/receipt events, cost snapshots and discrepancy decisions. Approval
and reservation must be concurrency-safe with POS and online order holds.
Extend the shared reservation/depletion claim constraints and adapters
intentionally; don't bypass the stock ledger. Post double-entry entries
atomically with physical movements and use one idempotency key per action.
A close decision needs complete quantity and cost reconciliation, not merely
a permitted actor. Distinguish authorization from successful reservation;
never claim stock reserved when allocation failed. Head office can choose
an alternative source before reservation; once reserved, cancel/release and
reauthorize before changing source. After dispatch use documented returns
or discrepancy resolution, not cancellation or editing historical cost.

No production stock, financial journal or database mutation is included in
this foundation slice. Full transfer workflow, branch purchase system,
ledger posting and integration tests remain outstanding.

## Persistent requisition and head-office decision implementation

The next slice adds InternalTransferRequest and InternalTransferRequestLine
with location/variant foreign keys, positive quantity checks, one variant
per requisition and a constrained requested/authorized/rejected lifecycle.
Authenticated endpoints (development contract, not yet public OpenAPI):
- GET /api/v1/inventory/transfer-requisitions: up to 100 newest requests,
  head office sees all, inventory managers see their active granted source
  or destination locations only.
- POST same route: destination, note and requested variant/base quantities;
  Idempotency-Key required, same normalized payload replays the record.
- POST /api/v1/inventory/transfer-requisitions/:id/decision:
  head-office admin selects a distinct active source and authorizes all
  requested quantities, or rejects with a reason. Identical decision
  retries replay; conflicting decisions fail. Actor and session are
  refreshed while the user row is locked; decision locks the request.
  Each new request or decision records an audit event in the transaction.

Authorization is not availability certification. All responses explicitly
mark stockReserved, stockMoved and financialPostingCreated false. No stock
or accounting mutation is allowed by these endpoints. Partial approval,
reservation, dispatch, receiving and accounting integration are next.
The schema is additive and has not been applied to the live database.

## Shared transfer reservation slice

An authorized requisition can now be reserved by the source location's
inventory manager or head-office administrator via POST
/inventory/transfer-requisitions/:id/reserve (version 2). The existing shared
allocator locks stock positions, observes POS and online order holds,
preserves safety stock and uses FEFO lots. Internal transfer allocations
are limited to lots with known nonnegative unit costs. Unknown cost is not
treated as zero; such lots are excluded from transfer allocation.
Authorization and successful reservation are distinct. The hold lasts 24
hours (initial server-defined policy) and changes no physical quantity or
financial balance. An expired or incomplete hold is reported as such and
cannot silently be renewed. Head office may cancel via POST
/inventory/transfer-requisitions/:id/cancel-reservation (version 3 and
nonempty reason); cancellation releases only that transfer's holds and
retains audit/history. A new requisition requires fresh authorization.
Database constraints validate source, requested variant, cost presence,
exact historical coverage and immutable reservation provenance. Transfer
reservation consumption is deliberately blocked until the dispatch
authority and accounting integration are implemented. The time limit,
cancellation/reissue process and costing policy are provisional defaults.

No migration is applied to a live database by this development change.

## Dispatch and immutable in-transit subledger

POST /api/v1/inventory/transfer-requisitions/:id/dispatch accepts reserved
version 3 and a physical handover reference. Source inventory manager
or head office must hold current authorization; no browser-supplied
quantity, source, destination or cost is accepted. Exact unexpired shared
reservation coverage is consumed by StockDepletionService. A single
transaction reduces the source lots, consumes the holds, records depletion
lines, stores immutable dispatch header and lot cost snapshots and sets
the transfer to in_transit, version 4. Repeating the same handover reference
does not deplete twice. The destination has not received goods yet.
Database deferred checks bind dispatch to the source, original reservations,
depletion and cost snapshots. The source valuation is the difference
between rounded before/after lot values, rather than rounding the dispatched
quantity independently; later receipt must allocate the exact stored
carrying value. GHS minor-unit values use integer arithmetic and are
serialized as strings. This slice creates an inventory valuation subledger,
not a general-ledger journal. No supplier payable, revenue, or tax posting.
No partial dispatch, receiver acceptance, discrepancy resolution, full GL,
or operational UI yet. Deployment and migrations remain gated on complete
integration testing and receipt/reconciliation readiness.

## Exact allocation of dispatched value to receipts

The receipt valuation helper allocates the immutable dispatch carrying value
proportionally to cumulative accepted quantities, using integer arithmetic.
Each receipt receives the difference of successive cumulative values, and
the final accepted quantity clears the exact original dispatch value.
The helper also records the difference between allocated value and nominal
rounded destination lot valuation. This difference is not revenue or profit;
future accounting integration must reconcile it explicitly. Missing or
damaged quantities remain outstanding until independently authorized
resolution. The helper alone does not create receipts, stock or journals.

## Read-only receipt reconciliation preview

The pure receipt preview accepts persisted dispatch lot identities, original
unit costs, exact carrying amounts and cumulative prior receipt evidence.
A receiving proposal identifies only dispatch lots and positive quantities.
The preview rejects unknown/duplicate lots, quantities beyond dispatch,
fractional piece units and inconsistent prior carrying values. It returns
the exact receipt and outstanding amounts, rounding differences and lot
expiry quarantine requirements. A complete physical receipt proposes
reconciliation, not automatic closure. Omitted lines remain in transit.

This is a testable domain helper, not a live endpoint or inventory mutation.
Database receipt integration, discrepancy authority, ledger posting and
operator screens are still required. The unfinished migration and request
schema are excluded from this commit.

## Independent discrepancy resolution preview

Head-office review requires an independently reported discrepancy with a
reason and evidence reference. The read-only domain helper distinguishes
destination quarantine, physical return to source and confirmed loss.
Proposed resolutions allocate only the outstanding original dispatch
carrying value. Quantity and value are conserved across prior accepted
receipts, previously resolved quantities and the new proposal. Zero-cost
goods still require full quantity reconciliation.

The initial staged design assumes ordinary accepted receipts precede
discrepancy resolutions. A future receipt service must use combined receipt
and resolution coverage before allowing further receipts after resolution.
The preview is not authorization to change inventory, recognize loss, post
financial journals or close the transfer; those actions require persisted
evidence, transactional controls and integration verification. Independent
reporter/reviewer is a proposed separation-of-duties rule.

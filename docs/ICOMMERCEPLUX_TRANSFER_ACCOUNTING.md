# iCommercePlux — Head-office-authorized transfers

The destination requests goods; head office chooses an active source (any
internal branch or warehouse) and authorizes quantities. A location grant,
even for both branches, is not head-office authorization. The current
foundation uses the existing admin role for head-office decisions, and
inventory_manager with location membership for stock-handling actions.
The next integration must load these facts from authenticated, persisted
records and check them again under transaction locks. The policy is not
an endpoint or database authority by itself.

## Accounting example (GHS)

100 cartons at original unit cost 25.00, total 2,500.00. No sales revenue,
supplier payable, VAT or new purchase is generated by the internal transfer.
Illustrative perpetual inventory control-account postings:

| Event | Debit | Credit | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Dispatch | Inventory in transit | Inventory, source branch | 2,500.00 |
| Accept 98 cartons | Inventory, receiving branch | Inventory in transit | 2,450.00 |
| Accept outstanding 2 cartons later | Inventory, receiving branch | Inventory in transit | 50.00 |

After the initial receipt the remaining two cartons and their 50.00 value
stay outstanding; missing, damaged or rejected stock needs independently
authorized resolution (return, recovery or justified loss/write-off).
A transfer must not silently close with unresolved quantity or value.
Separate actual freight/handling costs and any justified capitalization
under the company's accounting policy; never invent costs or markups.
Use the existing source lot's cost and expiry/batch provenance, not the
retail price. Unknown costs are blocked for valuation, not treated as zero.
The pure calculator uses integer thousandths and integer minor currency
units; cumulative rounding preserves the total across partial receipts.
Actual dispatch posting must reconcile source carrying value, including
fractional-lot rounding across multiple transfers and existing movements;
the calculator alone is not a full inventory costing engine.

## Integration gates

Persist a request, authorization, version, lines, lot allocations, immutable
dispatch/receipt events, cost snapshots and discrepancy decisions. Approval
and reservation must be concurrency-safe with POS and online order holds.
Extend the shared reservation/depletion claim constraints and adapters
intentionally; don't bypass the stock ledger. Post double-entry entries
atomically with physical movements and use one idempotency key per action.
A close decision needs complete quantity and cost reconciliation, not merely
a permitted actor. Distinguish authorization from successful reservation;
never claim stock reserved when allocation failed. Head office can choose
an alternative source before reservation; once reserved, cancel/release and
reauthorize before changing source. After dispatch use documented returns
or discrepancy resolution, not cancellation or editing historical cost.

No production stock, financial journal or database mutation is included in
this foundation slice. Full transfer workflow, branch purchase system,
ledger posting and integration tests remain outstanding.
